# DATA3800 Group 7 – Data Cleaning Summary

## Dataset

Source: City of Chicago Traffic Crashes

Study period: January 1 – December 31, 2025

Original dataset: 1,092,380 records across multiple years.

2025 records before target cleaning: 109,118.

Records excluded because of missing injury totals: 215.

Final dataset: 108,903 records.

## Target Variable

A binary target variable, `injury`, was created from `INJURIES_TOTAL`:

- 0: No recorded injury
- 1: At least one recorded injury

Final target distribution:

- Injury: 17,902
- No injury: 91,001

## Selected Predictors

1. POSTED_SPEED_LIMIT
2. WEATHER_CONDITION
3. LIGHTING_CONDITION
4. ROADWAY_SURFACE_COND
5. CRASH_HOUR

## Data Cleaning Decisions

- Restricted the dataset to crashes recorded in 2025.
- Removed records with missing or invalid injury totals.
- Checked for duplicate crash IDs; none were found.
- Converted numeric predictors to numeric types.
- Treated 198 zero speed-limit values as missing because we could not confirm that zero represented a valid posted speed limit. These crashes were kept in the dataset.
- Preserved UNKNOWN categories for weather (8,821), lighting (6,829), and road surface (15,362).
- Verified that crash hours were within the valid range 0–23.
- Preserved CRASH_DATE for chronological splitting.
- Did not impute missing predictor values or encode categorical features during cleaning.

Unusual nonzero posted speed limits were retained for further inspection.

## Leakage Prevention

`INJURIES_TOTAL` was used only to construct the binary target.

The five selected predictors exclude injury totals, injury severity, and related outcome information.

The target variable `injury` is provided separately as `y`, while the selected predictors form `X`.
